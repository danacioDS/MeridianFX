# Meridian FX

## **From Market Noise to Actionable FX Intelligence**

Foreign exchange markets generate enormous amounts of information every day: central-bank decisions, inflation, interest rates, employment, economic growth, volatility, commodities, capital flows, positioning, and thousands of financial documents.

The problem is not the lack of information.

**The problem is knowing which information matters, when it matters, and how it translates into a market view.**

**Meridian FX** is a quantitative financial intelligence platform designed to solve exactly that problem.

---

Meridian FX combines three major sources of intelligence:

### **Macro + Quant + Intelligence**

The platform analyzes:

* Macroeconomic data
* Interest-rate and yield differentials
* Global volatility
* Commodities
* Investor positioning
* Central-bank communications
* Machine-learning models
* Macroeconomic regimes

And transforms them into a simple question:

> **What is happening in a currency, where is the balance of probabilities pointing, and why?**

# The Real Product

Meridian is not designed simply to “predict the market.”

It is designed to identify **incremental information**.

Every model is evaluated against a fundamental benchmark:

### **Random Walk**

Then Meridian progressively adds information:

**Random Walk → Linear → Nonlinear → Market → Regime → RAG → Adaptive**

This allows the system to measure how much value each layer actually contributes.

That means Meridian can answer a more important question than:

> “Did the model get the prediction right?”

It can answer:

> **“What additional information does this system provide beyond what could already be explained by a basic benchmark?”**

---

# Built for Professional Finance

Meridian is designed around principles that matter in serious financial research.

### Point-in-Time Data

The system uses only information that was actually available at the time of prediction.

### No Look-Ahead Bias

Future information cannot contaminate historical predictions.

### Walk-Forward Validation

Models are evaluated under conditions that closely resemble real-world deployment.

### Explainability

Predictions can be decomposed into economic drivers using SHAP and other explainability techniques.

### Model & Prediction Registry

Every prediction can be traced through:

**Prediction → Model → Features → Data → Source**

### Economic Evaluation

Accuracy alone is not enough.

Meridian evaluates:

**Sharpe Ratio · Drawdown · Profit Factor · Transaction Costs · Stability**
